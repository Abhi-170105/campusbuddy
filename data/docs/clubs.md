# Student Clubs

## Technical clubs
The coding club, ByteBusters, meets every Wednesday at 4:30 pm in CSE Lab 3.
The robotics club, Gears, meets every Friday at 4:00 pm in the ECE workshop.
The design club, Pixel, meets every Thursday at 4:30 pm in the Design Studio.

## Cultural clubs
The music club, Sur, meets every Tuesday at 5:00 pm in the auditorium green room.
The dance club, Rhythm, practises on Mondays and Thursdays at 5:30 pm in the multipurpose hall.

## Starting a new club
To start a new club you need at least 15 student members and a faculty coordinator.
Apply to the Dean of Student Affairs with the club's aims and a list of planned activities.
