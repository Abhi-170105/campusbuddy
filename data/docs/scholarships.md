# Scholarships

## Merit scholarship
The top 3 rankers in each branch, in each year, get a 50% tuition fee waiver for the next year.

## Need-based scholarship
Students whose annual family income is below Rs. 2.5 lakh can apply for the need-based scholarship.
Applications are made on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) by 31 October each year.
