# Attendance Policy

Students must have at least 75% attendance in each course to be eligible for the Semester End Examination.
Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation with a valid medical certificate. The condonation fee is Rs. 1,000 per course.
Students with attendance below 65% in a course are detained and must repeat that course.
Attendance is updated on the student portal every Monday.
Attendance for approved college events (hackathons, sports, cultural fests) is given as on-duty (OD) when the faculty coordinator submits the OD list within 3 days.
