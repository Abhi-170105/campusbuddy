# Fee Payment

The tuition fee for the odd semester must be paid by 31 July, and for the even semester by 31 December.
Fees are paid online through the student portal using UPI, net banking or a debit card. Cash is not accepted.
A late fee of Rs. 100 per day is charged after the due date, up to a maximum of Rs. 3,000.
The fee receipt can be downloaded from the portal under "Payments" right after payment.
For fee-related questions, visit the accounts section, Admin Block ground floor, 10:00 am to 4:00 pm on weekdays.
