# Central Library

## Timings
The library is open from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm on weekdays.
On Saturdays the library is open from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. It is closed on Sundays.
During Semester End Examinations the library stays open until 10:00 pm.

## Borrowing
Each student can borrow up to 4 books at a time for 14 days.
Books can be renewed once online if no one else has reserved them.
The fine for late return is Rs. 2 per day per book.
A lost book must be replaced with a new copy, plus a processing charge of Rs. 100.

## E-resources
IEEE Xplore, Springer and NPTEL videos are available on the campus network through the digital library page.
