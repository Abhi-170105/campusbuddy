# Sports

The sports complex is open from 6:00 am to 8:00 am and from 4:30 pm to 7:30 pm.
The gym is free for all students who show a valid college ID card.
Sports equipment can be borrowed from the Physical Director's office by leaving your ID card.
The inter-branch sports meet is held every December.
Students selected for university or state teams get on-duty attendance for match days.
