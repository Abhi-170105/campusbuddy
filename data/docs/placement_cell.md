# Training and Placement Cell

## Eligibility
To register for campus placements a student needs a CGPA of at least 6.0 and no active backlogs.
Registration is done on the placement portal at the start of the 7th semester.

## One-offer policy
A student who accepts an offer with a package of 6 LPA or more cannot sit for further companies,
except "dream companies" that offer 12 LPA or more.

## Training
Pre-placement training (aptitude, coding and mock interviews) is held every Saturday from the 4th semester onwards.
The Training and Placement Office is on the 2nd floor of the Admin Block.
