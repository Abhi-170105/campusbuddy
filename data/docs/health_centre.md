# Health Centre

The health centre is on the ground floor of the boys' hostel block.
A nurse is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
The doctor is available from 10:00 am to 1:00 pm on weekdays.
For an emergency on campus, call the internal extension 2222 for the college ambulance.
Medical certificates for attendance condonation or make-up tests are issued by the health centre.
