# Hostel Rules

## Timings
All hostel residents must be back in the hostel by 9:30 pm.
Visitors are allowed only in the visitor lounge from 4:00 pm to 7:00 pm.

## Mess timings
Breakfast: 7:15 am to 9:00 am.
Lunch: 12:30 pm to 2:00 pm.
Snacks: 4:30 pm to 5:30 pm.
Dinner: 7:30 pm to 9:00 pm.

## Leave and conduct
To go home overnight, apply for leave through the warden at least 24 hours in advance.
Room heaters, induction stoves and other high-power appliances are not allowed in rooms.
Ragging in any form leads to immediate expulsion from the hostel.
