# College Transport

The college runs 12 bus routes across the city.
The annual bus pass costs Rs. 18,000 and is bought through the student portal.
The first buses leave the City Bus Stand at 7:00 am.
Buses leave the campus at 5:15 pm on weekdays and at 1:30 pm on Saturdays.
A lost bus pass can be replaced at the transport office for Rs. 300.
