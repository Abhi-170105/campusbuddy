# Grievances and Anti-Ragging

## Anti-ragging
Ragging is a punishable offence. Report any incident to the Anti-Ragging Committee.
The national anti-ragging helpline number is 1800-180-5522.

## Grievances
Students can raise a grievance through the "Grievance" section of the student portal.
Grievances are answered within 15 working days.
Complaints of harassment go to the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), which keeps them confidential.
