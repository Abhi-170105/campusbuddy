# Academic Calendar 2026-27 (Odd Semester)

## Key dates
Classes for the odd semester began on 4 August 2026.
Internal Assessment 1 (IA-1) is held from 15 September to 19 September 2026.
Internal Assessment 2 (IA-2) is held from 27 October to 31 October 2026.
The last working day of the odd semester is 28 November 2026.
The Semester End Examinations (SEE) start on 7 December 2026 and end on 23 December 2026.
Winter break runs from 24 December 2026 to 4 January 2027.
The even semester begins on 5 January 2027.

## Holidays
Deepavali holidays are on 9 and 10 November 2026.
Kannada Rajyotsava holiday is on 1 November 2026.
