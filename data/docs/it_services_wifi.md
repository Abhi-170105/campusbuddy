# IT Services and Campus Wi-Fi

## Wi-Fi
The student Wi-Fi network is called SIT-Students.
Log in with your USN and your student portal password.
Each student can connect up to 2 devices at the same time.
The daily data limit is 5 GB per student.

## Help desk
The IT help desk is in room B-012, open 9:00 am to 5:00 pm on weekdays.
Forgotten portal passwords can be reset from the login page using your registered email.
Every student gets a college email account in the first week of the first semester.
