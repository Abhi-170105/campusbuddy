# Examination Rules

## Hall ticket
A hall ticket is issued only to students with at least 75% attendance and no pending fees.
Hall tickets can be downloaded from the student portal one week before the Semester End Examination.

## In the exam hall
Students must report to the exam hall 20 minutes before the exam starts.
Mobile phones, smart watches and bags are not allowed inside the exam hall.
Only non-programmable scientific calculators are allowed.
A student who arrives more than 30 minutes late is not allowed to write the exam.

## Revaluation
Students can apply for revaluation within 7 days of the results being announced.
The revaluation fee is Rs. 500 per subject, paid through the student portal.

## Make-up internal assessment
A student who misses an internal assessment for medical reasons can apply for a make-up test within 5 days, with a certificate from the college health centre.
